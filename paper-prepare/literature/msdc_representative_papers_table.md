# MS-DC-ELT Representative Papers Table

| Category | Representative paper | Why representative | Problem solved | Core method | Relation to MS-DC-ELT | Limitation for current problem | Cite? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Online TbD baseline | SORT, Bewley et al., 2016, https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00763 | It defines the common lightweight online tracking-by-detection pipeline used by later SORT-family trackers. | Efficient online detection-to-track association. | Kalman prediction, IoU cost, Hungarian assignment, simple creation and deletion. | Establishes the base pipeline: detect, predict, associate, manage tracks. | Does not use low-confidence detections; lifecycle is mostly fixed heuristic; weak under missed detections. | Yes |
| Appearance-enhanced association | DeepSORT, Wojke et al., 2017, https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.07402 | It is the canonical SORT extension using appearance features. | Reducing ID switches under occlusion and similar motion. | Kalman motion gating plus learned ReID embedding and matching cascade. | Clarifies that ReID is a major way to improve ID continuity. | Current MS-DC-ELT has no ReID; do not imply appearance modeling as a contribution. | Yes, as boundary |
| Lifecycle decision model | MDP Tracking, Xiang et al., 2015, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2015/papers/Xiang_Learning_to_Track_ICCV_2015_paper.pdf | It explicitly models target lifetime with states and policies. | Online decisions for active, tracked, lost and inactive targets. | Markov Decision Process; data association in lost state; termination after lost timeout. | Directly related to track lifecycle management and lost-track decisions. | It learns policies and uses appearance/templates; not a dual-confidence detection evidence tracker. | Yes |
| Low-score detection association | ByteTrack, Zhang et al., 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06864 | It is the central prior for using low-score detections in online MOT. | Avoiding missed true objects and fragmented trajectories caused by discarding low-score boxes. | Two-stage association: high-score detections first, low-score detections second for unmatched tracklets. | Closest prior for using low-confidence detections. MS-DC-ELT can be positioned as moving from second-stage association to lifecycle evidence and delayed confirmation. | Low-score boxes mainly recover existing tracks; low-score detections do not usually start delayed low-confidence candidates with multi-frame confirmation. | Yes |
| Observation-centric motion recovery | OC-SORT, Cao et al., 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14360 | It is a recent strong motion-only online tracker and directly addresses missing measurements. | Kalman error accumulation during occlusion or missing measurements. | Observation-centric re-update, observation-centric momentum, recovery association. | Important comparison for short missing detections and lost-period recovery. | Focus is motion estimation under missing observations, not low-confidence detection evidence accumulation. | Yes |
| Motion, appearance and GMC baseline | BoT-SORT, Aharon et al., 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14651 | It is a strong practical baseline combining several association cues. | Robust association in multi-pedestrian MOT. | Improved Kalman state, camera-motion compensation, appearance ReID, motion/appearance fusion. | Defines a strong baseline family that MS-DC-ELT should compare against. | Uses ReID and GMC, which current MS-DC-ELT does not implement. | Yes, with boundary |
| Missing association and missing detection | StrongSORT++, Du et al., 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13514 | It explicitly names missing association and missing detection as MOT problems. | Tracklet fragmentation and missing detections. | StrongSORT plus AFLink for tracklet linking and GSI for detection interpolation. | Supports discussion of fragmentation reduction and missing-detection handling. | AFLink/GSI are post-processing/interpolation components; not equivalent to online lifecycle evidence update. | Yes |
| Confidence-aware cascade | ConfTrack, Jung et al., 2024, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2024/html/Jung_ConfTrack_Kalman_Filter-Based_Multi-Person_Tracking_by_Utilizing_Confidence_Score_of_WACV_2024_paper.html | It directly studies confidence score usage in Kalman-filter TbD trackers. | Noisy low-confidence detection causing ID switches and tracking failure. | Low-confidence penalization and cascade matching for tentative and confirmed tracks. | Highly relevant to low-confidence detection and track confirmation. | Target is multi-person/crowd MOT; it penalizes/uses confidence in association, not the same evidence-score state machine. | Yes |
| Confidence boosted association | BoostTrack / BoostTrack++, Stanojevic and Todorovic, 2024, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00138-024-01531-5 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13003 | Recent work explicitly uses detection-tracklet confidence and low-confidence detection boosting. | Better true-positive selection and association when confidence and similarity are imperfect. | Confidence-scaled similarity, soft confidence boost, shape/Mahalanobis/soft BIoU, varying thresholds. | Relevant to using confidence as a continuous signal rather than a hard threshold. | Focus remains association score design; does not define a separate low-candidate confirmation and inheritance lifecycle. | Yes |
| Weak-cue online association | Hybrid-SORT, Yang et al., 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00783 | Recent SORT-family method that uses confidence as one weak cue. | Ambiguous association when spatial and appearance cues degrade. | Adds velocity direction, confidence and height state to association. | Shows confidence can be useful beyond thresholding. | Not focused on delayed low-confidence target confirmation or lost-track inheritance. | Yes |
| Detection uncertainty | UTrack / UncertaintyTrack, 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.17098 and https://arxiv.org/html/2402.12303v2 | These papers treat detector uncertainty more explicitly than ordinary score thresholding. | Association under detection/localization uncertainty. | Predictive distribution or localization uncertainty integrated into association. | Useful to discuss future direction and limits of score-only evidence. | Requires uncertainty outputs from detector; current MS-DC-ELT uses detection scores and geometry only. | Optional |
| Geometry under irregular motion | C-BIoU, Yang et al., 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14317 | Represents geometry-only improvement when motion is irregular and appearance is not reliable. | Matching under irregular motion and similar appearance. | Cascaded buffered IoU. | Relevant because MS-DC-ELT currently also lacks ReID and depends heavily on geometry. | It assumes detections are sufficiently available; not mainly about low-confidence and missed detections. | Optional |
| End-to-end query trackers | MOTR / TrackFormer / TransTrack, 2021-2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03247, https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02702, https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15460 | Represents the alternative to explicit TbD association. | Learning temporal association with track queries or attention. | Transformer query propagation, tracking-by-attention, joint detection/tracking. | Useful for broad related work taxonomy. | Different training and inference paradigm; not a direct prior for current rule-based lifecycle design. | Briefly only |
