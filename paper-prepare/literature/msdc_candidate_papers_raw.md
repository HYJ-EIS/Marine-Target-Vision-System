# MS-DC-ELT Candidate Papers Raw List

This file records candidate papers found during the literature search. Abstracts are paraphrased from the cited paper pages and PDFs.

| ID | Paper | Source | Year | Abstract / Core Claim | Initial Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | Bewley et al., **Simple Online and Realtime Tracking (SORT)** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00763 | 2016 | Online tracking-by-detection with Kalman filtering and Hungarian association. The paper emphasizes that detector quality strongly affects MOT performance and reports very high update speed. | High. Foundational online TbD baseline. It provides track creation and deletion heuristics, but uses only high-threshold detections and simple geometry. |
| C02 | Wojke et al., **Simple Online and Realtime Tracking with a Deep Association Metric (DeepSORT)** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.07402 | 2017 | Extends SORT with learned appearance descriptors and nearest-neighbor matching in appearance space, reducing ID switches during longer occlusions. | High for association taxonomy. Less direct for MS-DC-ELT because current method has no ReID. |
| C03 | Xiang et al., **Learning to Track: Online Multi-Object Tracking by Decision Making** | CVF: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2015/papers/Xiang_Learning_to_Track_ICCV_2015_paper.pdf | 2015 | Models the lifetime of each target as a Markov Decision Process with states such as active, tracked, lost and inactive. A lost target can be kept, recovered by association, or terminated after a timeout. | High for lifecycle management. Older, but directly relevant to state-based tracking and lost-track decisions. |
| C04 | Bergmann et al., **Tracking without bells and whistles (Tracktor)** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.05625 | 2019 | Uses the detector regression head to propagate boxes and analyzes remaining MOT failure cases such as small objects, occlusion and missing detections. | Medium. Useful for showing that detector failures remain important. Current MS-DC-ELT does not use detector regression or ROI redetection. |
| C05 | Zhang et al., **FairMOT** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01888 | 2020 / IJCV 2021 | Jointly learns detection and ReID in a one-shot model and balances the detection and re-identification tasks. | Medium. Important MOT family, but not central to current rule-based lifecycle layer. |
| C06 | Zhou et al., **Tracking Objects as Points (CenterTrack)** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01177 | 2020 | Performs simultaneous detection and tracking by predicting object centers and offsets, avoiding a separate heavy association stage. | Medium. Useful boundary method. It is not a low-confidence lifecycle method. |
| C07 | Zhang et al., **ByteTrack: Multi-Object Tracking by Associating Every Detection Box** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06864; ECCV PDF: https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136820001.pdf | 2021 / ECCV 2022 | Associates high-score detections first, then uses low-score detections to recover unmatched tracklets and filter background detections by track similarity. Reports consistent IDF1 gains when BYTE is applied to multiple trackers. | Very high. Most direct prior for low-confidence detection association. |
| C08 | Cao et al., **Observation-Centric SORT (OC-SORT)** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14360; CVF PDF: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Cao_Observation-Centric_SORT_Rethinking_SORT_for_Robust_Multi-Object_Tracking_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf | 2022 / CVPR 2023 | Improves Kalman-filter TbD by using observations to reduce error accumulated during occlusion or missing measurements. Introduces ORU/OCM/OCR style observation-centric updates. | Very high for motion/lost recovery comparison. It addresses lost-period motion error, not low-confidence delayed confirmation. |
| C09 | Aharon et al., **BoT-SORT** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14651 | 2022 | Combines motion, appearance information, camera-motion compensation and an improved Kalman state vector for robust multi-pedestrian tracking. | High as strong baseline. Boundary: current MS-DC-ELT has no ReID or GMC. |
| C10 | Du et al., **StrongSORT / StrongSORT++** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13514; ACM: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1109/TMM.2023.3240881 | 2022 / TMM 2023 | Revisits DeepSORT and adds stronger detection, embeddings and association. StrongSORT++ adds AFLink for missing association and GSI for missing detection interpolation. | High for fragmentation and missing-detection discussion. AFLink/GSI are post-processing/interpolation, not current online MS-DC-ELT. |
| C11 | Seidenschwarz et al., **GHOST: Simple Cues Lead to a Strong Multi-Object Tracker** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04656; CVPR project: https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dvl/publication/ghost23cvpr/ | 2022 / CVPR 2023 | Studies whether simple tracking-by-detection with improved appearance and motion cues can compete with end-to-end trackers. | Medium. Useful for association taxonomy, less direct for low-confidence lifecycle. |
| C12 | Yang et al., **Cascaded-Buffered IoU (C-BIoU)** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14317; WACV PDF: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/papers/Yang_Hard_To_Track_Objects_With_Irregular_Motions_and_Similar_Appearances_WACV_2023_paper.pdf | 2022 / WACV 2023 | Proposes buffered IoU for objects with irregular motions and similar appearances. Expanding boxes helps maintain non-zero matching scores under motion. | Medium to high. Relevant to geometry-only association under difficult motion, not specifically detection confidence. |
| C13 | Maggiolino et al., **Deep OC-SORT** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11813 | 2023 | Adds adaptive ReID use to OC-SORT, improving motion-based association with appearance cues. | Medium. Strong baseline family, but current MS-DC-ELT has no appearance embedding. |
| C14 | Yang et al., **Hybrid-SORT: Weak Cues Matter for Online MOT** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00783; ACM/AAAI: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1609/aaai.v38i7.28471 | 2023 / AAAI 2024 | Adds weak cues such as velocity direction, confidence and height state to compensate for spatial and appearance ambiguity. | High for recent confidence-aware association. It uses confidence as a cue, not a separate delayed-confirmation lifecycle path. |
| C15 | Jung et al., **ConfTrack** | CVF: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2024/html/Jung_ConfTrack_Kalman_Filter-Based_Multi-Person_Tracking_by_Utilizing_Confidence_Score_of_WACV_2024_paper.html | WACV 2024 | A Kalman-filter tracking-by-detection tracker that penalizes low-confidence objects and uses cascading matching to handle noisy detections and tentative/confirmed tracks. | Very high. Directly related to confidence score, noisy detection and track confirmation. |
| C16 | Stanojevic and Todorovic, **BoostTrack** | Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00138-024-01531-5 | 2024 | Uses detection-tracklet confidence to scale similarity, favoring high-confidence detection-tracklet pairs in association. | High. Relevant to confidence-aware association, but not a low-candidate lifecycle method. |
| C17 | Stanojevic and Todorovic, **BoostTrack++** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13003 | 2024 | Improves BoostTrack with soft detection confidence boost, shape, Mahalanobis distance, soft BIoU and varying similarity thresholds for tracklets not regularly updated. | High and recent. Directly addresses low-confidence detections through score boosting and richer similarity. |
| C18 | Solano-Carrillo et al., **UTrack: Multi-Object Tracking with Uncertain Detections** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.17098 | 2024 | Incorporates empirical predictive distributions from object detection into MOT and introduces association methods that leverage detection uncertainty. | High for uncertainty-aware MOT. It needs uncertainty estimates beyond ordinary confidence scores. |
| C19 | **UncertaintyTrack: Exploiting Detection and Localization Uncertainty** | arXiv HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2402.12303v2 | 2024 | Adds extensions to tracking-by-detection trackers so localization uncertainty from probabilistic detectors can affect association. Reports fewer ID switches on BDD MOT when uncertainty is informative. | High for uncertainty, but not directly implementable unless the detector outputs localization uncertainty. |
| C20 | Zeng et al., **MOTR** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03247 | 2021 / ECCV 2022 | End-to-end Transformer MOT with track queries transferred frame by frame. It avoids explicit detection-to-track heuristics. | Boundary method. Important MOT direction, not the same problem formulation as MS-DC-ELT. |
| C21 | Meinhardt et al., **TrackFormer** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02702; CVF: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Meinhardt_TrackFormer_Multi-Object_Tracking_With_Transformers_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf | 2021 / CVPR 2022 | Transformer tracking-by-attention with track queries for identity-preserving predictions. | Boundary method. Relevant to related work, but not to rule-based low-confidence lifecycle management. |
| C22 | Sun et al., **TransTrack** | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15460 | 2020 | Uses Transformer query-key association and learned object queries to combine detection and tracking. | Boundary method. It reduces separate association machinery but does not directly answer low-confidence lifecycle decisions. |
| C23 | **Sentinel for confidence-aware multi-object tracking** | Nature Scientific Reports: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-43938-2 | 2026 | Introduces an uncertainty-aware tracker that diagnoses per-track uncertainty online and adapts the tracking strategy for confidence and track-loss issues. | Potentially high but very new. Needs careful full-paper reading before being treated as representative. |
| C24 | **Multi-object tracking review: retrospective and emerging trend** | Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-025-11212-y | 2025 | Reviews recent MOT development and divides methods into tracking-by-detection and end-to-end categories. | Medium. Useful for background framing, not a primary method source. |

## Search gap

The search did not find enough strong, directly matched literature on **UAV/USV sea-air small-target online MOT that specifically uses low-confidence detections, delayed low-confidence confirmation, lost-track reactivation, and lifecycle evidence accumulation together**. Existing close papers mostly study generic pedestrian MOT, autonomous-driving MOT, crowded-scene MOT, or 3D MOT.
